Before you begin

Quiz rules

  • Enter your full name before the quiz begins.
  • Every question accepts one written answer only. There are no multiple-choice options.
  • You have 20 seconds to submit each answer.
  • Submit one answer per question. You cannot change it after submission.
  • Spelling must match the official answer. Letter case, punctuation and extra spaces do not matter.
  • Short forms, abbreviations and alternative answers are not accepted unless they match the official answer after punctuation and case are ignored.
  • A correct answer earns 100 points in the first 5 seconds, 90 points in the next 5 seconds, 80 points in the next 5 seconds, or 70 points in the final 5 seconds. An incorrect answer earns 0 points.
  • Do not refresh or leave the page during a question. Keep this page open until the quiz ends.
The quiz has 50 questions. The master screen reveals the official answer after each question.
